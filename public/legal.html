Community Guidelines

Dunia connects strangers from every country. That only works if everyone feels safe. These rules apply to video, audio, chat and your profile.

You must be 18 or older

Dunia is for adults only. If you're under 18, don't use it. If someone you meet appears to be a minor, end the chat and report them with “Appears to be under 18”. These reports are treated as our highest priority.

Never involve minors in anything sexual

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material or any sexualisation of minors. Accounts are removed permanently and we report to the relevant authorities, such as [NCMEC / your national hotline], as the law requires.

No nudity or sexual content

Keep your clothes on and your camera appropriate. Don't expose yourself, perform sexual acts, or ask others to. Sexual solicitation, including selling content, is not allowed.

Respect people

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves.
  • No hate speech or hateful symbols targeting people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, disability, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation.
  • No violence, weapons used to intimidate, or graphic content.
  • Don't record, screenshot or share anyone without their consent, and never share someone's personal details.

No spam, scams or moving people off Dunia

Don't advertise, beg for money, run scams, or push people to other apps and websites. To protect you, links, phone numbers, e-mail addresses and social handles are hidden in chat automatically.

Be honest about who you are

People choose who to meet by gender and country, so tell the truth about both. Impersonating someone else, or lying about your gender or country, can be reported and gets you restricted.

How we enforce these rules

  • Skip, block, report. You can leave any chat instantly. Blocking means you'll never be matched again. Reporting also blocks.
  • What moderators see. When you report someone, moderators receive a still image of their video at that moment, their last few chat messages, and profile details. The person is never told who reported them.
  • Automatic restrictions. When several different people report the same person within 24 hours, their access is paused automatically (24 hours the first time, 7 days after that). Reports about minors or sexual content count more heavily.
  • Moderator review. Moderators can restrict for longer or permanently, or lift a restriction made in error.
  • Appeals. If you think you were restricted by mistake, e-mail [appeals@your-domain] with the time it happened.

Video and audio

Calls use WebRTC and are encrypted end to end between browsers (DTLS-SRTP). Most calls connect directly. When a direct connection isn't possible, the encrypted stream passes through a relay (TURN) server, which cannot decrypt it. We never record calls.

Cookies and storage

Dunia doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your settings and profile are kept in your browser's local storage. Fonts and flags are served from our own server, so no third party learns that you visited.
