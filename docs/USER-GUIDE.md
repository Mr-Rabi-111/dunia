# Dunia user guide

How to use Dunia, from your first visit to your hundredth conversation.

## 1. Your first visit

Dunia opens in your browser's language automatically (20 languages are available). You can change it any time
with the **globe button** at the top.

The first time, Dunia asks four quick things:

1. **Your name.** This is what other people see. Use your real first name or a nickname, 2–20 letters or numbers,
   in any script (राहुल, Анна, 明 and José all work). Stuck? Tap **Surprise me** for a random one.
2. **Male or female.** People can choose to meet only men or only women, so this must be honest. Lying about it
   can be reported.
3. **Where you're from and the languages you speak.**
   - Your country is usually filled in already from your connection. Tap it to search and change it, by typing
     in your own language or in English.
   - Add up to 3 languages you speak. Dunia prefers to connect you with people you can actually talk to.
   - Tick the box to confirm you're **18 or older** and agree to the Community Guidelines.
4. **Your camera.** Tap **Allow camera & mic** and accept your browser's prompt. You'll see yourself and a green
   meter that moves when you speak, so you know your microphone works.

Everything is saved on your device, so next time you go straight to the start screen.

## 2. Choosing who to meet

On the start screen, the card **“Who do you want to meet?”** has three filters:

| Filter | Options | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Gender** | Anyone · Men · Women | Women → you only meet women |
| **Country** | Anywhere, or any of 201 countries and territories | India → you only meet people in India |
| **Language** | Any language, or a language they speak | Japanese → you only meet Japanese speakers |

Plus optional **interests** (up to 5, such as Music, Gaming or Language exchange). Interests don't exclude anyone;
people who share one are just matched first.

**Filters work both ways.** You only meet someone if you fit *their* filters too. If you choose "Women in Japan",
you'll meet a woman in Japan who is also happy to meet someone like you. Nobody is ever pushed into a chat
they filtered out.

Under the start button you'll see **"People online who match: N"**, a live count for your current filters. If it shows 0,
widen a filter. When you search with filters and it's quiet for 20 seconds, Dunia offers **Search everywhere**.

The country list shows how many people are online in each country and puts the busiest countries at the top.

## 3. Talking

Tap **Start video chat**. A radar shows Dunia looking for someone; when it finds a person you'll hear a chime and
see their **name, flag and country** in the corner, the languages they speak, and interests you share. A green
**✓** next to their name means their country matches where they're actually connecting from.

### Controls

| Button | What it does | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| **Next** | Skip to a new person right away | `Esc` |
| **Stop** | End and go back to the start screen | — |
| Microphone | Mute or unmute (the other person sees a "Muted" badge) | `M` |
| Camera | Turn your video off or on | `V` |
| Switch camera | Front/back camera (phones with several cameras) | — |
| 😊 | Send a reaction that floats across the video | — |
| Chat | Open the text chat | `/` |
| Block · Report | See [Staying safe](#4-staying-safe) | — |

On phones, **swipe left on the video** to skip, and drag your own small video to any corner.

### Chat

Type below the video (on phones, tap the chat button). You'll see when the other person is typing. In recent
Chrome versions, messages in another language show a **Translate** button. Translation happens on your device,
so nothing is sent to a translation service.

Links, phone numbers, e-mail addresses and social handles are hidden automatically (shown as •••). This protects you
from scams and from people trying to move you somewhere unmoderated.

### When someone leaves

You'll see "<name> left", and Dunia finds someone new after 3 seconds. Tap **Back to lobby** to stop instead,
or turn off automatic searching in Settings.

## 4. Staying safe

- **Next** is always one tap (or `Esc`) away. You never owe anyone a conversation.
- **Block** means you'll never be matched with that person again. On phones it's inside the report sheet
  ("Block without reporting").
- **Report** (the flag button, or the flag on the person's name card on phones). Pick a reason. You're moved to
  someone new immediately, the person is blocked for you, and moderators get a still image of their video and
  their last few messages. **They're never told who reported them.**
- Report **"Appears to be under 18"** whenever someone looks like a minor. These reports are handled first.
- **Blur new video until I tap it** (Settings) hides each new person's video until you choose to look.
- Never share your address, workplace, passwords, money or explicit images, and be wary of anyone who asks you to
  continue on another app.

## 5. Premium, rewards and invites

**Everything is free except one thing:** choosing to meet *only men* or *only women*. Tap **Men** or
**Women** in the lobby and the Premium window opens.

1. **First time?** Tap **Try it free for 10 minutes**. The filter you picked switches on right away.
2. **Or choose a pass:** 15 minutes ₹1 · 1 hour ₹3 · 1 day ₹19 · 1 week ₹69 · 1 month ₹149.
   You pay once. **Nothing renews automatically.** Buying again adds time.
3. **Paying with UPI** (website and the downloaded app):
   - On a phone, tap **Open my UPI app** and pick Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or your bank app.
   - On a computer, **scan the QR code** with any UPI app on your phone.
   - After paying, type the **12-digit UTR / reference number** from the payment receipt and tap
     **I've paid — turn on Premium**. Premium starts immediately (for longer passes, the rest starts
     once the payment is confirmed, usually within a few hours).
4. **Paying in the Play Store app:** tap **Pay with Google Play** and confirm in Google's window.
5. The gold chip at the top shows how much Premium time is left. Two minutes before it ends you'll
   get a reminder with an **Extend** button. When it ends, your filter goes back to *Anyone*; chatting
   stays free.
6. **New phone?** Open the Premium window → **Restore a purchase** → enter your UTR or Google order ID.

**Free Premium minutes:**

- **Daily bonus:** have one chat longer than 30 seconds, then claim 5–30 free minutes. Come back
  every day to grow your streak (5, 5, 10, 10, 15, 15, 30 minutes).
- **Invite friends:** Rewards → **Share link**. When your friend joins with it and finishes their
  first chat, you *both* get 30 minutes.
- **Your world:** every new country you talk to is added to your collection of flags.

## 6. Settings

Open with the sliders button at the top:

- **App language** and **theme** (System, Dark or Light)
- **Camera, microphone and speaker**: choose the device if you have more than one
- **Blur new video**, **find someone new automatically**, and **sounds**
- **Edit profile**: change name, gender, country or languages (tapping your name at the top does the same)
- **Reset everything on this device**: removes your profile, settings and device ID from this browser

## 7. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Camera access is blocked" | Click the camera/lock icon in the address bar → allow Camera and Microphone → **Try again**. On iPhone: Settings → Safari → Camera/Microphone → Allow. |
| "Camera unavailable" | The page isn't on `https://`. Open the secure address. |
| "No camera or microphone found" | Close other apps using the camera (Zoom, Teams, another tab), then **Try again**. |
| "Couldn't connect. Finding someone else." | Your or their network blocked the connection. It retries with someone else automatically. If it happens every time, your network is strict; ask the site operator whether a TURN relay is configured. |
| Always waiting, nobody found | Your filters are too narrow for the moment. Use **Search everywhere**, or check the "People online who match" count. |
| Echo or feedback | Use headphones, or lower your speaker volume. |
| "Your access is paused" | Several people reported a break of the guidelines. It shows when you can return. If you think it's a mistake, contact the site's appeals address. |

## 8. Privacy in one paragraph

Video and audio go directly between you and the other person, encrypted, and are never recorded. Your profile lives
in your browser and is sent to the server only while you're connected. Chat messages are passed along, not stored,
except that when someone is **reported**, their last messages and a still image go to moderators and are deleted after
48 hours. The full details are in the site's Privacy page (`/legal#privacy`).
