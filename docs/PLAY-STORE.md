# Publishing Dunia on Google Play

This checklist walks through what Google Play requires of a random video chat app with in-app
purchases, and how Dunia meets each requirement. Policies change: confirm each item in
Play Console before you submit. Last reviewed September 2026.

---

## 1. Two builds, two ways to pay

| Build | Where it's distributed | How people pay for Premium | Plans offered |
|---|---|---|---|
| **play** (`app-play-release.aab`) | Google Play Store | **Google Play Billing** only | 1 day, 1 week, 1 month |
| **direct** (`app-direct-release.apk`) | Your website, other stores, sharing the file | **UPI** (any UPI app, QR + UTR) | all 5, including ₹1 / 15 min |
| website | `https://your-domain` | UPI | all 5 |

**Why the Play build doesn't show UPI.** Google Play's Payments policy requires apps distributed
on Play to sell in-app digital goods (Premium time is one) through Google Play Billing. In India,
Google also runs a **user choice billing** program: enrolled developers may show an alternative
payment option *next to* Play Billing, through Play's own user-choice API and with a reduced service
fee. That program needs enrolment in Play Console and extra integration (the Billing Library's
`enableUserChoiceBilling`), so it is **not** switched on in this code. The Play build sets
`upiAllowed = false` in `android/app/src/play/java/app/dunia/chat/StoreFactory.kt`.

**Why ₹1 / 15 min and ₹3 / 1 hour are UPI-only.** Google Play's minimum price for an in-app product
in India is ₹10, so these two passes can't be Play products.

## 2. Create the in-app products

Play Console → your app → **Monetize with Play → Products → One-time products** → Create:

| Product ID | Name | Suggested price (INR) | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| `dunia_pass_day1` | Premium – 1 day | ₹19 | Consumable (the app consumes it after the server grants time) |
| `dunia_pass_week1` | Premium – 1 week | ₹69 | Consumable |
| `dunia_pass_month1` | Premium – 1 month | ₹149 | Consumable |

Play converts prices for other countries; you can override them per country.

## 3. Let the server verify purchases

1. Google Cloud Console → create a **service account** → create a JSON key.
2. Play Console → **Users and permissions** → invite the service account's e-mail → grant
   *View financial data* and *Manage orders and subscriptions* for the app.
3. On the server, set:
   ```
   PLAY_PACKAGE_NAME=app.dunia.chat
   PLAY_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON=/secure/play-service-account.json
   ```
4. Restart. The startup line shows `play=verify`.

The server checks every purchase token with the Google Play Developer API (`purchases.products.get`),
requires it to be *purchased* and tied to the same device (`obfuscatedAccountId`), grants time once
per token, and only then does the app consume the purchase. Pending payments (e.g. cash / UPI inside
Google Play) are granted when they complete; unconsumed purchases are delivered on the next launch.

## 4. Store listing requirements

| Requirement | What to enter | Dunia |
|---|---|---|
| App category | **Communication** (or Social) | – |
| Target audience | **18 and over only**. Answer "no" to appealing to children | 18+ gate in onboarding |
| Content rating (IARC questionnaire) | Answer **yes** to "users can interact / exchange content" and to sharing of user-generated content; no violence/sexual content *provided by the app* | Expect Mature 17+ / 18+ ratings |
| Privacy policy URL | `https://your-domain/legal#privacy` | Filled template in `public/legal.html` (replace the [brackets]) |
| Account / data deletion URL | `https://your-domain/legal#delete` | In-app: Settings → Reset everything (also deletes server data) |
| Child Safety Standards (required for random chat & social apps) | Standards URL: `https://your-domain/legal#child-safety`; in-app reporting: Report → "Appears to be under 18"; child safety point of contact | Section in `public/legal.html` — fill in the contact |
| UGC policy | Terms accepted before chatting; in-app report & block; ongoing moderation | 18+ & terms checkbox; Report (7 reasons + snapshot); Block; `/admin` console; auto-restriction |
| Ads | "No ads" | – |
| App access | Explain that no login is needed: "Open the app, enter any name, pick gender and country." | – |
| Graphics | Icon 512×512, feature graphic 1024×500, 2–8 phone screenshots | `android/store-listing/` and `docs/screenshots/` |

## 5. Technical requirements

| Requirement | Dunia |
|---|---|
| Target API level | `targetSdk = 36` (new apps and updates must target Android 16 / API 36 from 31 Aug 2026) |
| Play Billing Library version | 8.0.0 (Play requires version 8 or newer from 31 Aug 2026) |
| App bundle | `bundlePlayRelease` produces the `.aab` Play requires |
| 64-bit | No native code, so nothing to do |
| Play App Signing | Upload with your **upload key**; Google holds the app signing key |
| App Links for invites | Set `ANDROID_CERT_SHA256` on the server to the **app signing key** SHA-256 from Play Console → App integrity. The server serves `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` |

## 6. Submission steps

1. Build: GitHub → Actions → *Android* → **Run workflow**, or locally (see [ANDROID.md](ANDROID.md)).
2. Play Console → Create app → fill in the listing, data safety, content rating, target audience,
   child safety standards and app access.
3. **Testing → Internal testing** → upload `app-play-release.aab` → add your own Google account as a
   tester and a **license tester** (Setup → License testing) so test purchases aren't charged.
4. Test on a real phone: onboarding, camera, a call between two phones, Report, Block, buying a
   week pass (license tester), Restore, the back button, rotating the phone.
5. **Closed testing**: new personal developer accounts must run a closed test with at least 12 testers
   for 14 days before production access. Organisation accounts don't need this.
6. Production → roll out to 10%, watch crashes (Android vitals) and reviews, then 100%.

## 7. Common rejection reasons for chat apps, and how to avoid them

- **Sexual content in screenshots or description:** use neutral screenshots (the fake camera ones are fine).
- **No working report/block:** the reviewer will try it. Make sure `/admin` is monitored.
- **Missing child safety standards / contact:** fill in the placeholders in `public/legal.html`.
- **Misleading claims:** don't claim "100% safe" or "verified users".
- **Payment policy:** the Play build must not show UPI or link to the website to pay.
